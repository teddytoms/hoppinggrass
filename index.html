How they're made

Every game starts as a question.

  1. 1

    Idea

    What would be fun? What should the player try to do?

  2. 2

    Drawing

    Ruhi draws the people, the places and the screens on paper.

  3. 3

    Rules

    How do you win? What costs money? What is fair?

  4. 4

    Game

    The rules become code. Then we play it, and fix what is not fun.

About

A small studio with big ideas.

Hopping Grass is where Ruhi makes games with her dad. She is the designer: the ideas, the characters and the rules are hers.

The games are free. There are no ads, nothing to sign up for, and nothing is collected. Your saved game stays on your own device.
